I'm Ioannis, a researcher and entrepreneur working across **artificial intelligence, biomedical engineering and clinical research**. My work connects technical development with the realities of healthcare.

I am **Co-founder & CEO of [Aisthesis Medical](https://aisthesismed.com/)**, where we develop AI decision support for predicting in-hospital complications, starting with sepsis. I lead multidisciplinary R&D across machine learning, clinical research, software development, medical-device quality and regulatory strategy, alongside commercialisation and business development.

I hold a **PhD in Clinical Medicine Research from [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/)** and an **MEng in Electrical & Computer Engineering from the [Technical University of Crete](https://www.tuc.gr/)**. My doctoral research focused on AI-enabled hyperspectral imaging and diffuse reflectance spectroscopy for gastrointestinal cancer surgery.

My experience also spans clinical data science at Humanitas Research Hospital, medical imaging at iThera Medical, computer vision at Perseptive, and neurotechnology research at NERF/imec. I have taught and supervised undergraduate and postgraduate students at Imperial College London and the Technical University of Crete.

## Research interests

* **Predictive AI in healthcare:** machine learning with clinical data for early prediction of complications and patient deterioration.
* **Optical sensing & surgical guidance:** diffuse reflectance spectroscopy and hyperspectral imaging for tissue characterisation and surgical margin assessment.
* **Computer vision & medical imaging:** image analysis, optical tracking and three-dimensional reconstruction for biomedical and surgical applications.
