My research spans predictive AI, optical sensing and computer vision, supported by clinical study design and evaluation.

## Predictive AI in healthcare

At [Aisthesis Medical](https://aisthesismed.com/), I lead the development and clinical translation of AI decision support for predicting in-hospital complications, starting with sepsis. This work brings together machine learning, clinical evaluation, healthcare software and hospital integration.

My earlier work at Humanitas Research Hospital focused on AI and machine learning for colorectal cancer prediction and inflammatory bowel disease detection, in collaboration with clinical and research teams.

## Optical sensing & surgical guidance

My PhD at Imperial College London investigated **a hyperspectral optical surgical tool for circumferential resection margin assessment in gastrointestinal cancer surgery**. The research combined diffuse reflectance spectroscopy, tissue classification and optical tracking to support real-time assessment during surgery.

Related collaborative work covers hyperspectral imaging, fluorescence-guided breast surgery, and computational modelling of light transport in tissue.

## Computer vision & medical imaging

My work includes surgical instrument and spectroscopy-probe tracking, three-dimensional tissue reconstruction, and depth estimation in laparoscopic images. Earlier projects addressed computer vision for human movement analysis and automated neuron segmentation in confocal microscopy at NERF/imec.
