My work connects AI research, clinical evaluation, product development and hospital integration.

## Current work

### Aisthesis Medical

**Co-founder & Chief Executive Officer · Since 2022**

I co-founded [Aisthesis Medical](https://aisthesismed.com/) to develop AI decision support for predicting in-hospital complications, starting with sepsis.

I lead multidisciplinary teams across machine learning, software engineering, clinical research, regulatory affairs and hospital implementation. Working with clinicians and hospital stakeholders, I translate clinical workflows into functional requirements, software specifications and R&D workstreams.

I oversee AI and software development, verification and validation, and retrospective and prospective clinical evaluations across European hospitals. This includes study design, clinical protocols, performance evaluation and coordination with clinical investigators.

Alongside R&D, I lead medical-device quality and regulatory strategy under EU MDR and US FDA pathways, within an ISO 13485-certified quality management system. I also lead commercialisation, fundraising and business development.

## Earlier experience

### Humanitas Research Hospital

**Senior Data Scientist · Milan, Italy · 2022–2025**

I developed and clinically evaluated AI and machine-learning models for colorectal cancer prediction and inflammatory bowel disease detection, combining computer vision and clinical data in collaboration with clinical and research teams.

My work covered data preparation, model development, validation, statistical evaluation and interpretation of results. I also co-designed clinical study protocols, assessed diagnostic performance and contributed to scientific and technical documentation, helping ensure that the models addressed clinically meaningful questions.

### iThera Medical

**Software Engineer Intern · Munich, Germany · 2021–2022**

I contributed to software R&D for biomedical imaging, supporting the development, testing and integration of software components used in imaging workflows.

Working alongside software, imaging and biomedical engineering teams, I contributed to technical problem-solving, software validation and documentation within an established medical-technology product-development environment.

### Perseptive

**Computer Vision Engineer · London, UK · 2020–2021**

I developed computer-vision and machine-learning methods for automated analysis of human movement, including applications related to frailty assessment.

I designed and evaluated image- and video-processing pipelines, from preprocessing and feature extraction through model development and performance assessment. I also translated research concepts into working prototypes, balancing algorithmic performance with practical deployment requirements.

### NERF / imec

**Research Intern · Leuven, Belgium · 2019**

At Neuro-Electronics Research Flanders (NERF/imec), I worked on deep-learning methods for automated segmentation of neurons in confocal microscopy images, applying biomedical image analysis to neurotechnology research.
